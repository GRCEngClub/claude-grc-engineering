# CMMC Level Selection — preliminary review

Ask for the actual solicitation or contract clauses, subcontract flow-downs, information types, boundary and desired assessment. Record unknowns as **not provided**. Do not infer a level solely from labels such as FCI, CUI, prime or subcontractor, or assume every Level 2 assessment requires a C3PAO. Cite the applicable provision and request qualified legal and assessor review.

The [DFARS 252.204-7025 provision](https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/dfars/html/current/252204.htm#252.204-7025) contains a contracting officer's level selection; review the actual contract and applicable deviations. [32 CFR 170.14](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.14) describes level requirements and [170.23](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.23) addresses subcontractors. [Current DoD program guidance](https://dodcio.defense.gov/cmmc/About/) reports Phase II suspended and Phase I continuing; check subsequent developments.

Output: cited contract language, known data and boundary, possible assessment type, missing information, open questions and reviewer. Never issue a certification or legal conclusion.
