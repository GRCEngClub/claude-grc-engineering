# CMMC Domain Guidance

Ask for the domain, level, contract and assessment scope. Verify practice IDs and exact text against [32 CFR 170.14](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.14) and the applicable [DoD guide](https://dodcio.defense.gov/cmmc/Resources-Documentation/). Do not silently substitute a different level or repair invalid IDs.

Level 2 uses NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2: AC Access Control, AT Awareness and Training, AU Audit and Accountability, CM Configuration Management, IA Identification and Authentication, IR Incident Response, MA Maintenance, MP Media Protection, PS Personnel Security, PE Physical Protection, RA Risk Assessment, CA Security Assessment, SC System and Communications Protection, SI System and Information Integrity. AM, RE, RM and SA are not Level 2 Rev. 2 families. For Level 1 or 3, consult the specific requirements and scoping sources rather than extrapolating Level 2.

For a Level 2 practice, use `/cmmc:evidence-checklist <practice-id>` and record source edition, candidate evidence, scope and unresolved questions. This is preliminary guidance, not an assessment or certification.
