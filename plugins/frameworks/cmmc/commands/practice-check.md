# CMMC Practice Check

Accept a practice ID and optional review focus. Preserve the ID as provided: if it is invalid, mismatched to its level or ambiguous, ask for clarification rather than repairing it. Verify the exact identifier and text in [32 CFR 170.14](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.14) and the applicable [DoD assessment guide](https://dodcio.defense.gov/cmmc/Resources-Documentation/). For example, Level 2 MFA is IA.L2-3.5.3; do not label 3.5.7 as MFA. Check Level 1 identifiers against the applicable official Level 1 guide.

Ask for contract, assessment type, scope, source edition, evidence and review period. For Level 2, use `/cmmc:evidence-checklist <practice-id>` to draft objective-level review. For Levels 1 and 3 consult level-specific sources. Report candidate activities, evidence references, limitations and open questions; missing evidence is **not assessed**. Do not assert implementation, pass/fail, certification, or a score without authorized human review.
