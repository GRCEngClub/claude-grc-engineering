# CMMC Level 2 assessment objectives

The prior local objective transcription and Rev. 2-to-Rev. 3 crosswalk were removed because they have not been fully verified and contained contradictory mappings and unsupported assessment claims. This skill is a source-navigation aid, not an authoritative transcription, automated assessment or certification.

1. Confirm the contract, Level 2 assessment type, assessment boundary and source edition. Record unknowns rather than guessing.
2. Consult [NIST SP 800-171A, June 2018](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-171A), incorporated by [32 CFR 170.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.2), and the [DoD Level 2 Assessment Guide v2.13, September 2024](https://dodcio.defense.gov/Portals/0/Documents/CMMC/AssessmentGuideL2v2.pdf). Check [DoD resources](https://dodcio.defense.gov/cmmc/Resources-Documentation/) for later versions.
3. Locate the exact requirement and lettered objectives. Cite the source and version; use `/cmmc:evidence-checklist <practice-id>` for a draft worksheet. Choose candidate examine, interview and test activities in context; do not claim that all methods are universally required.
4. Findings and scoring require qualified human review. Consult [32 CFR 170.24](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.24) for MET, NOT MET and N/A and [170.21](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-32/section-170.21) for conditional POA&M requirements. Do not treat a partially implemented requirement as a separate official finding category.
5. Do not infer Rev. 3 equivalence or map between revisions without a verified source and human review. Level 1 and Level 3 require their own applicable sources.
